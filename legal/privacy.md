Last updated: 17 November 2026

## Who we are

## The short version

- We don't save the text you paste, and we don't save your result.
- To check the text, we send it to Anthropic, an AI company. Before sending it, we remove phone numbers and personal profile links, and we hide the part of an email address before the @ (the domain stays, so a Gmail address can still be spotted).
- Anthropic deletes the text within 30 days and doesn't use it to train its models. The exceptions are below.
- We don't give you an ID. No sign-up, no email, no cookies, no analytics.
- Want to try it without pasting anything real? Use one of the made-up sample ads.

## What we handle and why

We use "legitimate interests" as our legal basis under the UK and EU GDPR: giving you the check you asked for, and keeping the service working and safe.

- **The text you paste.** Why: to check it. We don't save it. Anthropic holds the redacted text for up to 30 days (see below).
- **Your result (the verdict and warning signs).** Why: to show it to you. It exists only on your screen. We don't save it.
- **Your IP address.** Why: to limit each visitor to 20 checks an hour, so the service isn't overloaded or abused. It is held in memory only, never written down, and forgotten within the hour. It is also cleared whenever the app restarts.
- **Error messages.** If a check fails, the app records only the type of error, never your text or your IP address.
- **Basic web server logs, only if the app is hosted online.** If you run Job Ad Checker on your own computer, nothing is hosted and no server logs are kept by anyone else. If a public demo is offered, its host keeps basic logs for security and to keep the site running. These can include your IP address, the time and the page requested, but never your text, because the text isn't sent in the web address. The host and how long it keeps logs will be named here.

We never ask for your name, and we never sell your data.

## Anthropic, the AI service

- What it does: reads the redacted text and returns the warning signs it finds.
- Where: the United States.
- How long: Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days.
- Exceptions: if Anthropic's safety systems flag a message as breaking its usage policy, Anthropic can keep it for up to 2 years. It can also keep data for longer where the law requires it.
- Training: Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.

Anthropic's policy: privacy.claude.com, "How long do you store my organization's data?"

## Other services

- **Hosting.** None when you run the app on your own computer. If a public demo is offered, its host and region will be named here.
- **Google Fonts** supplies the page's font. When the page loads, your browser asks Google's servers for the font, which sends Google your IP address. Google says it doesn't use this to profile you.

## Transfers outside Switzerland, the EU and the UK

Anthropic is in the United States. Where data leaves Switzerland, the EU or the UK, it is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with the Swiss and UK addenda, in Anthropic's data processing terms.

## The Paste and Copy buttons

The **Paste** button reads your clipboard only when you tap it, and your browser may ask you to allow this first. We don't read your clipboard at any other time.

The **Copy** button puts your result on your own clipboard. Nothing is sent to us.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing [contact email]. We don't keep anything linked to you, so there is usually nothing to find or delete. For text Anthropic still holds, we can't single out your text, because nothing links it to you.

If you're unhappy with how we use your data, you can complain to:

- Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch)
- UK: the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)
- EU: the data protection authority in your country (list at edpb.europa.eu)
- Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au)
- Canada: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca)
- US: your state attorney general, or the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov)
- Anywhere else: your national data protection or consumer authority
